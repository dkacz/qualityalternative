HIGH — reviewed content is not cryptographically bound to the later import when documentSha256 is omitted.
Exact claim: A READY candidate can be reviewed against one Drive blob and later import a different Drive blob from the same contentFileId without forcing rescan or showing a changed-package state.
Why vulnerable: MainViewModel.scanAgentInboxDrive downloads content during review and computes an actual SHA-256 for duplicate and manifest-mismatch display, but AgentInboxReviewCandidate stores neither the reviewed SHA-256 nor the reviewed byte size. The importer only rejects a content mismatch when the manifest supplied documentSha256; that field is optional. This creates a concrete cloud-content swap attack: after the operator visually reviews and accepts a READY package, the Drive file can be changed before import, and the changed content is imported under the reviewed title/topics/priority state.

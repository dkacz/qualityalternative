## Pack Assembly Rules

- A future 10-item pack may include at most 4 candidates from one source family.
- A future pack may include at most 3 renderable candidates from the same author or work unless explicitly approved.
- Link-only rows remain external handoff metadata only.
- Renderable rows require manual rights and excerpt clearance before app integration.
- Each pack should preserve the product shape: one primary recommendation, finite backups, and no feed behavior.

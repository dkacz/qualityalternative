Reward prediction error is one reason checking behaviors become sticky. If the outcome is uncertain but occasionally rewarding, the system learns that another check might be worth it.

That is why the open itself matters so much. Before content has even been consumed, the brain has already entered a loop of expectation. Breaking that loop after ten minutes is harder than interrupting it during the first second.

A well-timed replacement does not need to overpower the entire platform. It only needs to create enough friction and enough meaning to redirect the first step.

Taste is trained by repetition. The things you reach for when you are tired slowly become the things that feel natural to want.

This makes the smallest defaults important. A low-value feed does not only occupy time; it teaches the mind what kind of stimulation counts as relief.

A better alternative does not have to be grand. It can be a paragraph, a claim, a story, or one well-made page that leaves the appetite cleaner than it found it.

Over time, the win is not merely less scrolling. It is a quieter sense of what is worth wanting.

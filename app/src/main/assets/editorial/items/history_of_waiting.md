Older cultures often treated waiting as an active practice rather than a dead zone. Waiting before speaking, replying, traveling, or acting signaled that judgment had not yet been outsourced to urgency.

Modern app ecosystems reverse that norm. They flatten the cost of checking and make immediacy feel natural. But immediacy is not neutral. It quietly trains the body to confuse availability with relevance.

Even a short deliberate pause restores contrast. It reminds the user that not every possible action deserves execution the moment it becomes possible.

People often fail at self-regulation when the substitute feels too ambitious. "Read a book instead" is noble advice, but it is often the wrong unit of action for an impulsive moment.

Small bets work because they lower activation energy. A five-minute article, a short chapter excerpt, or one tightly written argument is often enough to change direction without triggering resistance.

In product terms, the best replacement is not the ideal one in theory. It is the one the user will actually accept when the habit is strongest.
